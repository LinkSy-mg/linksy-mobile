# Screenshots

Linksy for Android 0.3.3, for the website. The deck, What's New, the update sheet, the
computer's page and Settings were retaken on 2026-11-03 for 0.3.3; the rest are from
0.3.1 (2026-11-02), unchanged since. All from a clean install (phone named "Pixel 9")
with two paired computers: **Studio Desktop** (a Linux desktop with a picture, its
battery and music playing) and a **ThinkPad** (not nearby), plus **Living Room PC**,
nearby but not paired. 1080 × 2400 PNG; `deck-landscape.png` is 2400 × 1080 and
`music-notification.png` a 1080 × 506 crop. Light unless the name says `-dark`.

Desktop's "Work" layout): pages of tiles, the music playing there, a clock, the volume.
